      What counts

      Full meal 4 hours

      Anything cooked and substantial. If you sat down and ate a proper plate, it is a meal.

      • Rice, roti, bread, pasta, noodles
      • Dal, sabzi, curry, soup with grains
      • Breakfast plate, lunch, dinner
      • Pizza, sandwich, burger, dosa, idli with sambar
      • A large portion of anything mixed, even if it is called healthy
      Snack 2.5 hours

      Light or small portions.

      • Fruit: apple, banana, a handful of berries
      • A handful of nuts or dry fruit: almonds, walnuts, soaked peanuts, cashews, raisins
      • Biscuits, rusk, toast, chivda, a small sandwich
      • Yogurt or curd, a small bowl
      • Dates, a few soaked almonds

      Sweets and cold treats count as a snack:

      • Ice cream, one scoop or a small cup
      • Chocolate, a few squares or a small bar
      • Cake, cookie, mithai, halwa, dessert
      • Milkshake, a thick smoothie, lassi

      A large ice cream bowl, a big chocolate bar, or dessert after a meal counts as a full meal.

      Beverage 1.5 hours
      • Tea, coffee, green tea
      • Juice, milk, buttermilk
      • Protein shake, a Sanjeevani-type drink
      • Soft drink, energy drink
      Nuts quick rule
      • A small handful of nuts, dry fruit, or soaked groundnuts is a snack: 2.5 hours.
      • A large handful with banana porridge or nut-butter toast is closer to a meal: 4 hours if it felt filling.
